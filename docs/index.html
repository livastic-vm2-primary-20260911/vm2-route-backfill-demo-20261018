03 / FAILURE MODES TESTED

What this demo deliberately does not do

  • Never sends customer messages or claims a real booking. Its did_send_outreach output is false.
  • Rejects unschedulable jobs with a reason instead of silently dropping them.
  • Rejects an opted-out identity or shared location even if the other duplicate row looks eligible.
  • Rejects contradictory duplicate customer history and invalid duration fields conservatively.
  • Rejects fractional, Boolean, string or malformed route-matrix travel minutes instead of underestimating the driving time.
  • Does not send identifiable service addresses to a public OSRM routing server. Real data requires a buyer-authorized, privacy-preserving route source and explicit contract scope.
  • Does not prove global optimum, driving traffic conditions, compliance, live dispatch or a paid contract.
